GradeDesk
Offline grade recorder for William V.S. Tubman University

WHAT IT DOES

GradeDesk does the grade sheet you already do in Excel, without the formulas.
You type each student's raw marks across the class list, one assessment at a
time. GradeDesk looks every score up in the university transmutation table,
averages them into class standing, applies the weights, works out the final
grade and the letter, and exports the sheet you submit.

WHY YOU MIGHT WANT IT

  - You type marks instead of building and maintaining formulas.
  - Attendance scores itself from the register you mark.
  - It catches mistakes a spreadsheet will not: a score above its maximum,
    a missing exam that forces an I, blanks quietly counting as 50.
  - The exported record and summary open in Excel ready to submit, with no
    reformatting.

YOUR DATA

Everything stays on this computer, in a single file. Nothing is ever sent
anywhere, and no internet connection is needed at any point. Scores are saved
the moment you leave the cell, so a power cut costs you nothing already typed.

You can back up all your work to one file and carry it to another machine.

ACCURACY

The grading model was rebuilt from a real completed semester and checked
against it: 167 students across 6 course sections, every final grade and every
letter reproduced exactly, with no mismatches. That check runs automatically
on every build.
