# Video kit

Everything needed to make an instructor walkthrough video, without a camera.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md) | The timed script. Nine scenes, what is on screen, and the narration word for word. Also holds the full narration as one block, and a table of facts the video must not get wrong. |
| [AI_VIDEO_PROMPT.md](AI_VIDEO_PROMPT.md) | One long brief to paste into an AI video tool, with the seven screenshots attached. Includes which kinds of tool work and which fight you. |

Both are written for a 6 minute 30 second video at about 150 words per minute.

## The short version

The video is narration over screenshots. There is no presenter and nothing to
film. The images live in [`../images/`](../images/) and are already free of
real student names.

If you want the best result for the least trouble: record the app yourself with
[OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) while clicking through it, then use an AI
tool for the voiceover and captions only. A shot list for that is at the end of
`AI_VIDEO_PROMPT.md`.

## Before publishing

- Fill in a real contact where the script says `[contact]`.
- Check the narration against the facts table at the end of `SCRIPT.md`,
  especially if an AI tool rewrote any of it. The rules about blank
  assessments, blank exams and the accuracy claim are the ones that get
  garbled.
- Watch it once with the sound off, reading only the captions.
